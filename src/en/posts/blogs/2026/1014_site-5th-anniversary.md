Let's look back on these past five years. In the initial phase, including myself, two people built the site and wrote articles.

The two authors at launch grew to five by May 2022, reaching 100 articles, and by June they recorded a monthly high of 36 articles, gaining momentum. In August 2022, they surpassed 200 articles, and by year-end had grown to 28 authors (over 10 active).

By the end of 2023, the article count reached 522, surpassing 500 exactly two years after the site's launch. Search traffic also steadily increased, reaching 50,000 clicks per month in March 2023, boosting our recognition.

Growth continued, and by September 2025 we had surpassed 800 articles and 71 authors. As of September 2026, we have reached 912 articles and 83 authors. This milestone reflects the accumulation of articles leveraging each person's interests, concerns, and expertise.

### Expansion of Content Areas
At launch, we started with only a few categories such as "Containers" and "Microservices", but over five years the themes have expanded significantly as follows.

- **2022**: Microservices, Learning, Agile Development, IoT, Front-end, Robotics
- **2023**: Modeling, Machine Learning

From hardware-oriented fields like robotics, IoT, and embedded systems, to development process areas such as agile, quality assurance, and project management, and all the way to machine learning and generative AI, the lineup reflects Mamezou's diverse business domains.

### Evolution of the Site Infrastructure and Features
We have made steady improvements to enhance the experience for both readers and authors. We added many features, including a table of contents, image magnification, author avatar display, on-site search (later replaced by Algolia), tag search, support for mathematical formulas (KaTeX), recommended articles, and weekly access rankings.

We also went through a major technological shift in our static site generator, migrating from Eleventy to the Deno-based [Lume](https://lume.land/).

### Our Journey with Generative AI
These five years have also been a journey alongside the evolution of generative AI.

- In 2023, we launched the front-page column 'Mameka's Trivia' using GPT-3. Since then, Mameka has undergone successive generational updates with GPT-4, GPT-4 Turbo, and GPT-4o (writing this article reminded me that we forgot to update to GPT-5 😅).
- In 2024, we added a feature to automatically generate sidebar illustrations for articles using DALL·E 3. Mameka also plays an active role internally by commenting on article PRs and notifying us of access statistics.
- In 2024, we began automatically generating English translations of articles (now many articles are available in English), and in 2025 we added Chinese translations, gradually increasing access from overseas.

I believe that the active integration of generative AI into the operation of our in-house media is also a characteristic unique to this site.

### Annual Events and Community Building
Initiatives to broaden the range of contributors have also become established.

- **Advent Calendar**: First held in 2022 (16 participants, 7 of whom were first-time contributors), and since then has become an annual year-end tradition.
- **Summer Relay Series**: First held in 2023 (14 posts, 11 new contributors, including sales staff and new graduates debuting).
- **Spring Series for Newcomers**: First held in 2024, creating a platform for articles aimed at new engineers and project managers.

Through these initiatives, members who normally do not write have been given an opportunity to debut, contributing to the current increase in the number of authors.

### Integration with Social Media and Other Channels
Starting with the launch of the official X (formerly Twitter) account, we've expanded our communication channels to Bluesky, Facebook, and note. Authors have also had opportunities to present at external conferences such as JJUG CCC, creating a virtuous cycle where articles lead to external presentations.

We also feature highlight articles on Mamezou's news page.
- [Tech Updates – Mamezou MAMEZO](https://mamezo.tech/pub/news/techinfo/)

### Impact on Sales and Recruiting
By continuously writing articles, the developer site has also come to function as a platform for engineers to showcase their individual skills and areas of expertise. In fact, there have been cases where clients who read articles have reached out to authors by name, leading directly to new orders.

In mid-career recruiting, more candidates say they read the developer site articles, and we feel that our technical publications are definitely yielding results in recruitment as well.

## Future Prospects
As we celebrate the 5th anniversary, the developer site is already gearing up for a redesign. It will be reborn as a more readable and modern site. Aiming for a UI that offers "minimal noise and maximal focus on technical information" for most readers, we plan to enhance article page readability and add native support for dark mode. We will also proceed with the implementation process itself using the latest AI-Driven Development approach. By positioning the site as an "internal project for the practical deployment of generative AI," we intend to take another step forward in our journey with generative AI.

Regarding content, we aim to further enrich it by not only providing development tips but also enhancing topics we are working on and those likely to interest our readers, such as robotics and generative AI. We will continue annual initiatives like the Advent Calendar, the Summer Relay Series, and the Spring Series for Newcomers, continually creating opportunities for members who have not yet debuted as writers. Additionally, by expanding our English and Chinese translations, we want to deliver Mamezou's technical content not only domestically but also to engineers overseas.

Over the next five and ten years, we want to continue nurturing the site as a "useful resource for development" and as a media outlet that conveys the technical capabilities and character of Mamezou as a company.
